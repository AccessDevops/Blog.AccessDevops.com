Object storage is the quiet backbone of most modern stacks. Yet the tooling around it is still stuck in 2012 — flat listings, manual pagination, and a terminal full of `aws s3 ls` calls.

This blog is where we write down what we actually learn running infrastructure: the S3 quirks, the DevOps trade-offs, and the small automation wins that save hours.

## What to expect

- Practical, hands-on notes — no fluff.
- Honest comparisons between tools and providers.
- The occasional deep dive when something deserves it.
